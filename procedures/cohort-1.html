COHORT 1 · 🧠 AI & Market Foundations

📈 Can explain why Anthropic/Claude is growing faster than Microsoft or Google

🎯 Objective
Be able to give a two-minute explanation of Anthropic's growth relative to competitors, backed by numbers.
✅ Key Results
  • Can state Anthropic's approximate YoY growth rate vs. Microsoft's and Alphabet's
  • Can name at least one structural reason (developer focus, agentic adoption) driving that gap
📋 Prerequisites
  • None — this is a research task
🪜 Steps
  1. Search for recent Anthropic/Claude revenue or usage growth figures
  2. Compare against Microsoft and Alphabet's reported YoY growth for the same period
  3. Ask an AI assistant to summarize why the growth rates differ
  4. Write 2-3 sentences in your own words and save them to your notes
🔗 References
  • Cohort 1 session notes
  • Anthropic
COHORT 1 · 🧠 AI & Market Foundations

🖥️ Understand the difference between CPU and GPU processing for AI

🎯 Objective
Understand why GPUs, not CPUs, power modern AI model inference and training.
✅ Key Results
  • Can explain sequential (CPU) vs. parallel (GPU) processing in one sentence
  • Can name one reason GPUs suit token/matrix operations better than CPUs
📋 Prerequisites
  • None
🪜 Steps
  1. Ask an AI assistant to explain CPU vs. GPU processing "for a beginner"
  2. Generate or find a simple infographic contrasting the two
  3. Relate it back to why large language models need GPU clusters to run
🔗 References
  • Cohort 1 session notes
  • Glossary: Token
COHORT 1 · 🧠 AI & Market Foundations

🪙 Understand tokens, context windows, and input vs. output tokens

🎯 Objective
Understand how AI models measure and charge for the text they read and write.
✅ Key Results
  • Can define "token", "context window", "input token" and "output token" without notes
  • Can explain why a larger context window costs more to run
📋 Prerequisites
  • None
🪜 Steps
  1. Read the Glossary entries for Token and Context Window
  2. Send a short prompt to an AI tool and check the token count it reports (usually in usage/billing settings)
  3. Note how many tokens were input vs. output for that single exchange
🔗 References
  • Glossary: Token
  • Glossary: Context Window
